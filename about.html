Guru Kripahi Kevalam

A naam-jap and prayer-counting app for daily spiritual practice

Guru Kripahi Kevalam (also known as "Radha Naam Jap" / "Lalu-Chotopushu") is a free mala-counting and prayer-tracking application built for practitioners of the Radha Vallabh Sampraday( Harivangsi Sampraday ) and Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, with a focus on naam jap (chanting the holy names).

What the app does

  • Counts your daily jap / mala rounds with a simple tap counter
  • Tracks daily, weekly, and milestone targets (including a "28 Names" practice mode)
  • Shows a Vedic Panchanga (spiritual calendar) alongside your practice
  • Optionally syncs your progress across devices when signed in with Google
  • Lets you export your practice data at any time

Who it's for

Anyone maintaining a regular naam-jap or prayer-counting practice — the app works fully without an account, and signing in is only needed if you want your counts to sync across multiple devices.
