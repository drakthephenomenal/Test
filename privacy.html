Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the app Guru Kripahi Kevalam (also known as "Lalu-Chotopushu", "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is a naam-jap / prayer-counting application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — if you sign in with Google or Zoho, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider.
  • Usage data — jap counts, targets, and streak history you create within the App, stored so your progress can sync across devices.
  • Device permissions — the App may request notification and battery-optimization permissions to deliver reminders; it does not access your contacts, camera, or location unless a specific feature you enable requires it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To sync and back up your jap counts and settings across devices.
  • To send you optional reminder notifications.
  • To operate account sign-in and export features you request.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) services. You may export or delete your data at any time from within the App's settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Sign-In and, optionally, Zoho Sign-In for authentication. These providers have their own privacy policies governing the data they process.

Your Choices

  • You can use the App without signing in ("ghost mode" limitations may apply).
  • You can export your data at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to drakthephenomenal@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
